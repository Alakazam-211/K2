# v0.9.7 evidence: live typing (the hunk channel, E-LIVE r2.3)

Every figure cites the run that printed it (`[run JOB/RUN]` or `[run JOB/RUN:field]`: the run
records in `docs/evidence/runs/`, checked by `tools/evidence/check.py`). What v0.9.7 ships for
live typing: `Session::edit_at_base` (a live edit made at a base version, carried through every
change since and applied as one change), `Session::events_since`, the rebase rules
(`session::rebase`, amendment E-LIVE r2 with clarifications r2.1 and r2.3), the JS client
(`client/`, K1) that shares them, and person undo grouping (K4, undo spec U3).

**The rules as of r2.3**, which every figure here is checked against:
- the change applied first stays left at a tie;
- deletion vs deletion never conflicts;
- others' inserted text is never deleted (a deletion is split around it, and a replacement's
  text goes at the left end);
- `MPC-E111` only for competing text: ours' replacement meeting a range a change since deleted
  or replaced, or ours' insertion strictly inside a deleted range;
- **each hunk is carried as sent** (r2.3; r2.2's "re-canonicalize at every step" withdrawn);
- **"strictly inside" under both tie-break placements** (r2.3 S3);
- **a change that rebases to nothing applies nothing and takes no version** (r2.3 S4).

Kit commits: the kit's property, mutants, replays and grouping class ran at `b665cf4`. That
is kit main `0732ff9` plus this branch's run-record lines, the same commit as `e1dd118` after
the branch was rebased onto main `9d41958`, which changed only `client/`. The client's tests ran
at `9d41958` plus their run-record lines (§5). The rules are kit main's `fdf60ce` (carry as
sent) and `fd3c146` (S3).

## 1. The kit's property: clients converge, and refusals match intent

`crates/base/tests/live_edit.rs`, `live_clients_converge`, node job `756c5145`
(`--cpus 4`, `LIVE_CASES=10000`, `LIVE_THREADS=4`, debug build).

- **What runs:** each history has 3 clients against a real session. Each client models the JS
  client: one change in flight, the rest buffered, incoming changes carried through its pending
  ones by the client mirror. They type random hunks at random times, with random delivery and
  ack timing. Agent edits, the agent's undo and outside edits to the file land in the window too.
- **What is checked, after every step:**
  - **P1:** at every ack the client's prediction of its rebased change is the server's, and at
    the end every client's text is the server's;
  - **P3:** others' inserted text is never deleted;
  - **P4:** a refusal changes nothing;
  - **the window** is exactly the hunk sets broadcast;
  - **P2 and P2c, by intent:** every live edit is judged by an **ID-level oracle** that never
    uses the positional rebase (K2 Engine's, `g1-live-draft.md` §2, with its two tie-break
    markers per insertion for S3). A refusal must be a conflict by intent (P2), and every
    conflict by intent must be refused (P2c).
- **Streams:**
  - **mixed:** every kind of hunk;
  - **overlapping:** edits of one or two characters on a short text, so ranges overlap. This
    stream reaches the shapes r2.2's rule got wrong;
  - **inserts only** and **deletes only:** never refused.

## 7. What this does not cover

- **Not covered in the kit's property or in K2 Engine's model:**
  - the window's expiry (`MPC-E112`). Only the client's tests cover it (§5, bases expire);
  - rates (`MPC-E901`);
  - undo grouping inside K2 Engine's model. §6 covers it on the kit, against the undo spec's
    oracle.
- **D1** (authored ranges widened to tokens) is planned for v0.9.7. It merges into main after
  v0.9.6, and v0.9.7 ships with its promise sentence. The figures above ran before the merge,
  on a pack whose tokens are characters, where D1 is the identity. D1's evidence is in its own
  note (`undo/d1`: 20,000 traces, 0 disagreements) and is added here at merge time.
- **3×2 is sampled, not exhaustive,** in K2 Engine's model (`g1-live-draft.md` §0.2).
- **"Strictly inside" is decided:** reading (a), both tie-break placements (r2.3 S3), which
  v0.9.7 ships. The base-neighbour reading (b), the E-LIVE r2.4 candidate, was modelled too.
  Both keep every check in K2 Engine's model (`g1-live-draft.md` §0.5), and (b) was not
  adopted.
