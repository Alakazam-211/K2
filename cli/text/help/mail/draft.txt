Save a DRAFT into an inbox's Drafts folder. It never sends. Two forms:

  Reply draft (any inbox you have 'draft' on: K2-hosted, or a linked
  Gmail/IMAP/Microsoft 365 account): threaded onto the message (In-Reply-To,
  References, "Re:" subject, addressed to its sender; with --reply-all, to
  everyone on it too; with --to, to whoever you name instead). A reply to a
  message your inbox SENT goes to that message's To (its Cc too with
  --reply-all), not back to your inbox. It shows at the top
  of its thread in K2's Mail page, marked "Draft, not sent", where a person
  can edit it, send it (by your workspace's send rule) or discard it.
  'k2 mail drafts' shows what became of it.

  Compose draft (linked inboxes only): a brand-new To/Subject in your
  human's own Gmail/IMAP Drafts, for them to finish in their mail client.

  Delete a draft you saved: 'k2 mail draft delete <draft-id>' (hosted:
  removed from Drafts; linked: moved to Trash). A draft someone else saved
  is refused (not_your_draft, exit 3) and nothing is touched.

A draft takes up to 10 addresses in To + Cc and up to 500 in Bcc (a notice
to many members: put them in --bcc). Sending keeps the cap of 10 over
to + cc + bcc. A slow save is safe to repeat: each save carries an
idempotency key, and the same key answers the same draft (the CLI waits
and asks again by itself; on a timeout it prints the key to pass with
--idempotency-key).

Sending yourself is 'k2 mail reply' / 'k2 mail send' (level 'send' and the
workspace's Sending rule). A hosted reply draft takes no --attach yet (it
answers a usage error, exit 2): draft the text, and if a file must go,
send it yourself with 'k2 mail reply <id> --attach <path>' or ask your
human. Your level on each inbox (read < draft < send): 'k2 mail inboxes'.

USAGE
  k2 mail draft <message-id> (--body <text> | --body-file <f>) [--to <addr> | --reply-all] [--cc <addr>] [--bcc <addr>] [--attach <path>] [--idempotency-key <k>] [--json]
  k2 mail draft --reply-to <message-id> (--body <text> | --body-file <f>) [--to <addr> | --reply-all] [--cc <addr>] [--bcc <addr>] [--json]
  k2 mail draft --to <addr> --subject <s> (--body <text> | --body-file <f>) [--cc <addr>] [--bcc <addr>] [--attach <path>] [--from <linked>] [--idempotency-key <k>] [--json]
  k2 mail draft delete <draft-id> [--json]

ARGUMENTS
  <message-id>           Reply form: a message id from 'k2 mail messages'
                         (mutually exclusive with --to/--subject)

FLAGS
  --reply-to <id>        Reply form, spelled as a flag (same as <message-id>)
  --reply-all            Reply form: also address everyone on the original
                         (its To join the draft's To, its Cc the draft's Cc),
                         without your own inbox's addresses and repeats
  --to <addr>            Compose form: recipient (repeatable). Reply form:
                         who the reply goes to INSTEAD of the original's
                         sender (the thread stays); not with --reply-all
  --subject <s>          Compose form: subject (required with --to)
  --cc <addr>            Cc (repeatable or comma-separated). On a reply draft
                         it adds to the reply's recipients.
  --bcc <addr>           Bcc (repeatable or comma-separated). Saved as the
                         draft's Bcc; the mail client keeps it blind when
                         the draft is sent. Up to 500 on a draft (To + Cc
                         max 10).
  --from <linked-addr>   Compose: which linked inbox (required when several)
  --body <text>          The draft text
  --body-file <f>        Read the draft text from a file
  --attach <path>        Workspace-relative file to attach (repeatable; 10 / 25MiB;
                         linked inboxes only)
  --idempotency-key <k>  The save's key (8-128 of letters, digits, . _ : -).
                         Default: a fresh one. The same key again answers
                         the draft already saved (replayed: true), never a
                         second copy
  --json                 JSON output ({ok,id?,draftId?,address,folder,hint,
                         replayed?}): id = the message the draft answers
                         (m_…); draftId = the draft itself (d_…), as
                         'k2 mail drafts' lists it

ERRORS
  inbox_busy (exit 2)       the linked inbox already has 4 mail sessions open;
                            retry after retry_after_s, and run fewer at once
  inbox_throttled (exit 3)  the provider is limiting the account; wait
                            retry_after_s. Linking it again won't help
  not_your_draft (exit 3)   delete: that draft wasn't saved by your agent
