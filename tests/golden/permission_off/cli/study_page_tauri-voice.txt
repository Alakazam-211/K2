k2 study tauri-voice — calls inside a Tauri app's window

The rest of the Tauri side (updates, signing, links, push): k2 study tauri.

Two ways to put a call in the app:
- THE WEBVIEW (most Apps): your page runs Sew's browser voice kit
  (@sew/voice: WebRTC, a noise filter in an AudioWorklet, WebAssembly)
  inside the window: WKWebView on macOS, WebView2 on Windows. Your server
  gets the call credential (sewcc_…); the page talks to
  https://<your App's host>/_k2/voice/sew/ and to Sew's TURN.
- A NATIVE PLUGIN: Sew's tauri-plugin-sew-voice runs the call in Rust, with
  no WebRTC in the webview; its JS API follows @sew/voice where it can. A
  remote page reaches it over Tauri's IPC: a capability naming the page's
  URL (remote.urls), and ipc: http://ipc.localhost in connect-src. Ask Sew
  for its guide. K2 hasn't tested it for an App.
For the webview, in the Tauri repo's root:
  k2 app init --template tauri [--app <app>] --voice
It writes these (never over a file that is there; it prints what it can't):
- src-tauri/Info.plist (macOS): NSMicrophoneUsageDescription and
  NSCameraUsageDescription (without them WKWebView has no
  navigator.mediaDevices at all), and NSAppSleepDisabled (no App Nap).
  Info.ios.plist: the two usage keys. Android: the lines are printed.
- src-tauri/Entitlements.plist and bundle.macOS.entitlements:
  com.apple.security.device.audio-input and .camera (hardened runtime).
- tauri.conf.json, first window: backgroundThrottling "disabled" (macOS
  14+; without it a hidden window's page stops), and additionalBrowserArgs
  for WebView2 (audio without a tap, no slowing while hidden or covered).
- src-tauri/src/k2_voice.rs: the mic and camera for your App's own origin
  with no second prompt; any other page is refused. Wire it once in
  src-tauri/src/lib.rs (Tauri 2.12 or newer):
    mod k2_voice;
    tauri::Builder::default()
        .on_permission_request(k2_voice::on_permission_request)
  Add each custom domain the window loads to k2_voice::APP_ORIGINS.
- k2/README.md: the CSP your server sends while the room is on, e.g.
    script-src 'self' 'wasm-unsafe-eval'; worker-src 'self' blob:;
    connect-src 'self' https://<host> wss://<host>
  Serve the kit's worklet and .wasm from your own origin (.wasm as
  application/wasm). CSP doesn't cover TURN. 'wasm-unsafe-eval' needs
  macOS 13 or newer.
Windows: the person turns on Settings > Privacy > Microphone > "Let
desktop apps access your microphone". Calls answer on every live host of
your App with Sew 0.7.2+ and behindK2Front; otherwise only on the primary
(k2 app domain primary <app> <host>; k2 study app-chat).
WHAT K2 HAS TESTED: tests/app-template/tauri-voice-check builds the
--voice files into a stock Tauri 2.12.5 app, signs it ad hoc with the
hardened runtime, loads a page from a remote origin with the CSP above and
keeps the window hidden.
  macOS 26.4 (Mac mini M1,   passed: mediaDevices; WebRTC between two peers
  no screen, no mic)         through an E2EE frame transform, 50 packets/s for
                             10 s while hidden; the AudioWorklet with
                             WebAssembly inside it; WebAssembly under
                             'wasm-unsafe-eval' (refused without it); audio
                             without a tap; IndexedDB kept across a restart.
                             Without backgroundThrottling the hidden page
                             stopped. A hidden page's JS timers still slow
                             (about 3 a second): media doesn't, so keep call
                             logic off setInterval.
                             Not yet: the mic itself (the prompt, capture,
                             allow and refuse by origin: unit tests only), a
                             minimised window, a real call through Sew.
  Windows (WebView2)         compiles; never run
  iOS, Android               not tested
  Linux                      no: Ubuntu and Arch ship the webview without
                             WebRTC; open the call in Chrome
ON A REAL MAC (Rust installed, a mic; from a K2 checkout):
  K2_CHECK_MIC=1 sh tests/app-template/tauri-voice-check/run.sh
macOS's microphone prompt shows about 15 s into run 1: click Allow within
30 s. Every check line must say PASS (the mic lines too), then ALL
REQUIRED CHECKS PASSED. Then by hand, in your own app: join a call,
minimise the window for 2 minutes and talk from a phone; you keep hearing.
To be asked again: tccutil reset Microphone.
ON WINDOWS (Rust installed, a mic, the privacy switch on): in PowerShell,
in tests\app-template\tauri-voice-check\src-tauri:
  $env:K2_CHECK_MIC="1"; $env:K2_CHECK_PORT="47123"; cargo run
No prompt shows (the app allows its own origin). The same PASS lines.
Browsers: Chrome, Edge, Chromium yes (Sew tests these); Safari 15.4+ yes,
not tested by Sew yet; Firefox no (refused on an encrypted call).
