k2 onboarding — what K2 does with a workspace's own harness files
(CLAUDE.md, GEMINI.md, .cursor/rules and the like) when it is added

  k2 onboarding scan [<workspace-path>]          list its harness files with your own content
                                                 (none listed: nothing to decide)
  k2 onboarding adopt <file> [<workspace-path>]  seed .k2/PROJECT.md from that file; the file
                                                 is archived in the workspace, then removed
  k2 onboarding defer [<workspace-path>]         leave its harness files alone for now;
                                                 K2 still writes its own files
  k2 onboarding start-fresh [<workspace-path>]   archive them and use K2's own instead

<workspace-path> is the current folder when left out. later and fresh are
the old names of defer and start-fresh.
