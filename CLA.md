lets Alakazam Labs ship your contribution in K2 under its open-source licence, and also under other terms,
including commercial ones, without asking you again each time. You keep the copyright in your contribution, and
you can still use it however you like.

**How to sign.** The first time you open a pull request, a bot asks you to sign. Reply on the pull request with
this exact comment:

> I have read the K2 CLA and I agree to it.

You sign once, and it covers all your future contributions to K2. The record keeps only your GitHub username,
your GitHub account number, the pull request and the date.

**Contributing for a company?** See [Contributing on behalf of an organisation](#contributing-on-behalf-of-an-organisation)
below.

## Contributing on behalf of an organisation

If You contribute as part of Your job, or for an organisation that owns what You create, the organisation is the
copyright owner and must agree too. Either:

- the organisation confirms in writing that it has waived its rights in Your Contributions to Alakazam, or
- a person authorised to bind the organisation signs this Agreement for it, by posting this comment on the pull
  request (or by email to hello@alakazamlabs.com, with the same words):

  > I have read the K2 CLA and I agree to it on behalf of [organisation name], and I am authorised to do so.

  When an organisation signs, "You" in this Agreement means that organisation. It covers Contributions submitted
  by its employees and contractors on its behalf. The organisation agrees to tell Alakazam when someone should no
  longer be treated as contributing for it.

Every individual who submits a Contribution still signs for themselves as well.
