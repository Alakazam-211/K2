k2 vault reveal NAME [--scope <s>]

People only, from your own terminal or SSH login (not a K2 terminal):
show a value after a confirm (Touch ID or the vault password).
Away from the computer? Over SSH the confirm also goes to your phone, as
a card; anywhere else it goes there when nobody answers the computer's
prompt. Confirm it with your owner login's password; the first answer
wins and the card waits 5 minutes. The card goes on the Thread of the
workspace you run it in, else this server's own agent's (or
K2_VAULT_CONFIRM_THREAD=<workspace>); the terminal says which.
K2_VAULT_CONFIRM_METHOD=phone asks only the phone, =local never.
Asks at the computer only: making a login an owner or setting an owner
login's password, a systemd credential or a rollback of one, and
approving an agent's clone proposal.

Example: k2 vault reveal STRIPE_KEY

K2's own secrets are never shown, except Sew's launch key (the key Sew's
chat and voice data is sealed with), which the server's owner may see:
  k2 vault reveal sew-launch-key --scope engine:sew
Admins, Members and agents are refused (Admins and Members can still use
terminal sessions, which run as this server's user).
