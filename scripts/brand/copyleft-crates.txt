# Crates a private white-label build can't ship, and bundled C we accept.
# scripts/brand/licence-gate.sh reads it. It refuses a partner brand's
# build (any brand id but k2 and k5) when the tag's Cargo.lock holds a
# refused crate. K5's own builds only get a report.
#
# A refused crate, three fields split by " | ":
#   crate | why a private white-label build can't ship it | what lifts it
# An allowed crate (never refused; the gate names it in its report):
#   allowed crate | its licence | the ruling
# The refusal prints all three fields. Take a refused row out only when
# what lifts it has landed and the crate is gone from Cargo.lock.
libgit2-sys | libgit2 is GPL-2.0 with a linking exception and its xdiff part is LGPL-2.1, statically linked: accepted in public builds only, until gix (owner ruling) | the gix migration lands (K5 replaces libgit2 with gitoxide)
allowed pg_query | PostgreSQL License | accepted everywhere (owner ruling)
allowed libsqlite3-sys | SQLite is public domain | accepted everywhere (owner ruling)
